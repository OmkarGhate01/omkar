The Future of Work in the Age of AI Agents

Every generation has its "machines will take our jobs" moment. The spinning jenny. The assembly line. The personal computer. Each time, the nature of work changed — but humans didn't disappear. What's different about AI agents is the speed of change, and the breadth of what's affected.

The question is not "Will AI take jobs?" The question is "Which tasks will AI do, which will humans do, and who will manage the AI that does the rest?"

What AI Agents Are Actually Good At

AI agents excel at tasks that are: high-volume and repetitive (1000 calls, 10,000 data entries), pattern-based (classify this complaint, extract this data), available-24/7 (no sleep, no sick leave), and consistent (same quality on the 10,000th as the 1st).

They struggle at: genuine emotional intelligence, navigating truly novel situations with incomplete information, physical dexterity, creative direction (as opposed to creative execution), and ethical judgment in ambiguous situations.

Skills That Stay Safe

✓ High-Value Skills

  • AI system design & deployment
  • Complex problem framing
  • Relationship management
  • Ethics & governance
  • Creative direction
  • Cross-functional leadership

⚠ Automatable Tasks

  • Routine customer calls
  • Basic data entry & reporting
  • Template-based content writing
  • Basic coding (CRUD apps)
  • Document summarisation
  • Scheduling & reminders

The New Role: AI Orchestrator

The highest-value human role in the next decade isn't "AI expert" — it's "AI orchestrator." Someone who understands enough about AI to design workflows, delegate tasks to agents, monitor outputs, fix errors, and bring the whole system back to business goals. This is what I do, and demand for it is growing faster than supply.

The India Opportunity

India has a structural advantage here. We have enormous demand for economic growth, a young population comfortable with technology, and a long history of process outsourcing. The transition isn't from "India handles manual tasks for the world" to "AI handles those tasks instead." It's from "India handles manual tasks" to "India builds and runs the AI that handles those tasks globally."

The BPO worker who understands AI agents is worth 5× the one who doesn't. That upskilling window is open right now.

What to Do Starting This Week

Pick one repetitive task you do every day. Research whether an AI tool can handle part of it. Start with the easiest 20%. You'll learn more from that experiment than from any course. Then expand. The person who experiments fastest learns fastest — and that is a permanent competitive advantage in a world where the tools change every six months.
