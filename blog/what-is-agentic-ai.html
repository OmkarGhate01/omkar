What Is Agentic AI? The Next Big Shift in Artificial Intelligence

By Omkar Vijay Ghate  ·  2026-10-15  ·  LinkedIn  ·  All Articles

When ChatGPT launched in late 2022, it felt like magic. You could ask it anything and it would answer. But there was a fundamental limitation: it was reactive. You talked to it, it talked back. The conversation ended there.

Agentic AI is something completely different. An AI agent doesn't just respond — it plans, decides, takes action, checks the result, and tries again if something went wrong. It operates like a digital employee who can actually get things done, not just advise you on how to do them.

What Makes an AI "Agentic"?

The word "agentic" comes from "agency" — the capacity to act independently. An agentic AI system has four core properties that distinguish it from a regular chatbot:

  • Goal-directed reasoning: It breaks complex goals into sub-tasks and figures out how to accomplish each one.
  • Tool use: It can call APIs, search the web, read files, send emails, update databases — using real tools to take real actions.
  • Memory: It remembers context from earlier in a conversation or across sessions, so it doesn't start from scratch every time.
  • Self-correction: If a step fails, it recognizes the error and tries a different approach.

Think of it this way: a chatbot tells you what to do. An AI agent actually does it.

A Real-World Example from India

Let me show you what this looks like in practice. I built a voice AI agent for a TVS Motors dealership network in Maharashtra. When a customer calls at 11 PM — after the showroom has closed — here is what happens:

  1. The agent picks up the call in under 2 seconds.
  2. It greets the customer in their preferred language (Hindi, Marathi or English).
  3. It understands what they need — a test drive booking, a service enquiry, a price quote.
  4. It checks the dealer's calendar in real time and offers available slots.
  5. It books the appointment, updates the CRM, and sends an SMS confirmation to the customer.
  6. It logs a summary of the call for the sales team to review in the morning.

No human was involved. No customer was put on hold. No lead was lost because the office was closed. That is agentic AI working in the real world.

How Agentic AI Is Different from Previous AI

The evolution has been fast. In 2020, AI was primarily used for classification — is this email spam or not? Is this image a cat or a dog? In 2022-2023, large language models (LLMs) like GPT-4 changed the game with natural language understanding. But the real breakthrough has been in 2025-2026 with agentic frameworks that let LLMs actually execute multi-step workflows.

Here is the progression:

  • Classifiers (2018-2022): AI reads and categorises. Output: a label.
  • Generative AI (2022-2024): AI reads, writes, and suggests. Output: text or images.
  • Agentic AI (2025+): AI plans, acts, uses tools, and completes work end-to-end. Output: a done task.

The Key Technologies Behind Agentic AI

Building an AI agent requires several components working together:

1. A Large Language Model (LLM) as the brain

The LLM — typically GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, or Gemini 1.5 — handles reasoning, language understanding, and decision-making. It is the intelligence at the core of the agent.

2. Tools and APIs

Tools are what give the agent hands. Without tools, an LLM can only talk. With tools, it can search Google, call your CRM API, send WhatsApp messages, book calendar appointments, execute code, and much more.

3. Memory systems

Short-term memory (within a conversation) and long-term memory (across conversations) allow the agent to build context over time. This is what makes agents feel genuinely intelligent rather than forgetful.

4. An orchestration layer

Frameworks like LangChain, CrewAI, n8n, Bolna, and Vapi handle the orchestration — ensuring the agent executes tasks in the right order, handles failures gracefully, and knows when to escalate to a human.

Why India Is Uniquely Positioned for Agentic AI

India has a specific set of conditions that make agentic AI not just useful, but necessary:

  • Labour-intensive industries: Call centres, field sales, customer support, and lead qualification employ millions but operate at relatively low efficiency. AI agents can handle the high-volume, repetitive tasks, freeing humans for complex and relationship-driven work.
  • Multilingual population: India has 22 official languages and hundreds of dialects. Agentic AI systems trained on Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada can serve customers in their mother tongue — something no single human team can cost-effectively do.
  • Mobile-first behaviour: India has over 700 million smartphone users who are comfortable with voice interfaces. An AI agent that answers on WhatsApp or a phone call meets customers exactly where they already are.
  • SME market size: India has over 63 million micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs). These businesses cannot afford full-time support staff but desperately need customer-facing automation. Agentic AI makes enterprise-grade customer experience affordable at ₹5,000–₹20,000 per month.

The Indian AI agent market is projected to grow at 35% CAGR through 2028. Companies that build agentic AI capabilities today will have a 3–5 year head start over competitors who wait.

Test drive booking, service appointment scheduling, follow-up calls on enquiries, and customer satisfaction surveys after a vehicle service. TVS Motors and Force Motors are already using voice AI agents for these workflows.

Retail and E-commerce

The Business Case: Numbers That Matter

I have measured the impact of AI agent deployments across multiple clients. Here are the typical outcomes:

  • Response time: From 4–8 hours average to under 30 seconds, 24/7.
  • Lead conversion rate: Increases of 20–35% when AI handles the first touch within 60 seconds of an enquiry.
  • Cost per customer interaction: Drops from ₹80–₹200 (human agent) to ₹4–₹12 (AI agent).
  • Agent capacity: One AI agent can handle 500+ simultaneous conversations. A human call centre of equivalent capacity would require 50+ employees.

What Agentic AI Cannot Do (Yet)

It is important to be honest about current limitations:

  • Complex emotional situations: A customer who is deeply distressed needs human empathy. AI agents should detect emotional escalation and hand off smoothly.
  • Novel, unprecedented situations: If something genuinely outside the training data occurs, the agent needs a human fallback path.
  • Physical tasks: AI agents operate in the digital world. They cannot physically inspect a car or hand you a document.
  • Trust in high-stakes decisions: Medical diagnoses, legal advice, and large financial decisions still require human oversight, at least for now.

How to Get Started with Agentic AI

If you are a business owner or decision-maker reading this, here is the practical path forward:

  1. Identify your highest-volume, most repetitive customer interactions. These are the best candidates for AI agent automation. Look for tasks that involve answering the same 20 questions, booking appointments, sending reminders, or collecting information.
  2. Choose the right platform. For voice agents in India, Bolna AI and ElevenLabs work well. For WhatsApp automation, AiSensy combined with an LLM backend is powerful. For no-code multi-step workflows, n8n is excellent.
  3. Start with a pilot. Pick one workflow — say, incoming enquiry handling — and deploy an AI agent for it. Measure response time, customer satisfaction, and conversion rate before and after.
  4. Iterate and expand. Once the pilot proves ROI, add more workflows and more channels. The marginal cost of scaling an AI agent is near zero.

The Bottom Line

Agentic AI is not a future technology. It is here, it is working, and businesses across India are already using it to serve customers better, operate leaner, and grow faster. The question is not whether to adopt agentic AI — it is whether you want to be ahead of the curve or scrambling to catch up in two years.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between agentic AI and a chatbot?

A chatbot responds to questions with pre-scripted or LLM-generated text. An agentic AI goes further: it can take actions using tools — booking appointments, updating databases, sending messages, calling APIs — and it can chain multiple steps together to complete a full workflow without human intervention.

How much does it cost to build an AI agent for a small business in India?

A basic AI agent for a small Indian business typically costs ₹15,000–₹50,000 to build and ₹5,000–₹20,000 per month to run, depending on call volume and the platforms used. This is dramatically cheaper than the equivalent human staffing cost.

Which industries in India are best suited for agentic AI?

Automotive dealerships, real estate developers, financial services (mutual funds, insurance, lending), healthcare appointment booking, e-commerce customer support, and any business with high inbound enquiry volume are excellent candidates for AI agent deployment.

Do I need to know coding to use agentic AI?

No. There are excellent no-code platforms like n8n, Bolna AI, and Vapi that allow you to build sophisticated AI agents without writing code. However, a technical partner can help you integrate these with your existing systems like a CRM or booking platform.

Is agentic AI safe to use with customer data?

Yes, if built correctly. Good AI agent architecture keeps customer data within your own infrastructure, uses encrypted communication, and never stores sensitive information like payment details within the AI system itself. Always work with a developer who understands data privacy requirements under India's DPDP Act 2023.
