Voice Cloning for Business: How Indian Companies Are Creating AI Voices in 2026

By Omkar Vijay Ghate  ·  2026-10-10  ·  LinkedIn  ·  All Articles

Three years ago, creating a realistic AI voice required millions of dollars in R&D and months of recording studio time. Today, a Mumbai startup can create a custom AI voice that sounds indistinguishable from a human professional — for under ₹5,000 per month — and use it to handle thousands of customer calls simultaneously.

This is the voice cloning revolution that is quietly transforming how Indian businesses communicate with their customers.

What Is Voice Cloning?

Voice cloning is the process of creating a synthetic voice that mimics a real person's speech patterns, tone, accent, and emotional qualities. Modern AI voice cloning systems can create a convincing clone from as little as 3–10 minutes of audio recording.

The technology works by analysing hundreds of characteristics of the original voice — pitch, rhythm, pronunciation patterns, breath placement, resonance — and learning a mathematical model that can reproduce those characteristics when given new text to speak.

The result: a digital voice that can say anything you type, in real time, that sounds like the original speaker — or like a custom AI persona you have designed from scratch.

Two Types of Voice AI for Business

Type 1: Cloned Voices (Real Person's Voice)

You record a real person — a brand ambassador, a company founder, or a professional voice actor — and create an AI version of that voice. This clone can then be used at unlimited scale without the original person being present.

Use cases: A founder's voice for personalised customer welcome calls. A celebrity brand ambassador's voice for marketing campaigns. A professional voice actor's voice for IVR systems, with the ability to update scripts instantly without re-recording.

Type 2: Designed AI Voices (No Real Person Required)

Platforms like ElevenLabs, Murf AI, and PlayHT let you design a voice from scratch — specifying gender, age, accent, warmth, pace, and professional quality — without cloning any real person.

Use cases: A company creates "Priya" — an AI voice agent that sounds like a warm, professional Indian woman — for all their customer support calls. This is not any real person; it is a designed persona that the company owns and controls entirely.

Ethical Considerations: Critical for Indian Businesses

Voice cloning is powerful — and that power comes with responsibility. Here is what ethical use requires:

Always Disclose

Under India's Consumer Protection Act and emerging TRAI regulations, businesses should disclose when a customer is interacting with an AI voice. The disclosure can be brief and natural: "Hi, this is Priya, an AI assistant from [Company Name]." Customers appreciate honesty more than being tricked.

Get Consent Before Cloning a Real Voice

If you are cloning a real person's voice — even a willing employee or brand ambassador — get written consent that specifies exactly how the voice will be used, at what scale, and for how long. This is both ethical and legally prudent.

Do Not Impersonate

Using a voice clone to impersonate a real person — a government official, a company executive, or a public figure — without their consent is illegal in India under the IT Act and potentially the IPC. This is a serious line that must never be crossed.

Provide a Human Escalation Path

Every AI voice interaction should have a clear path for the customer to reach a human agent if they need one. A customer who cannot reach a human when they genuinely need to is a customer who files a complaint and never comes back.

How to Implement Voice Cloning for Your Business: Step by Step

  1. Define your use case. Is this for inbound calls, outbound reminders, IVR menus, or WhatsApp voice notes? The use case determines the platform and architecture.
  2. Choose a platform. For Indian languages and accents, Sarvam AI or ElevenLabs. For conversational voice agents (not just playback), Bolna AI. For content creation and marketing, Murf AI.
  3. Create or record your voice. If designing from scratch, use the platform's voice designer. If cloning a real person, record 5–10 minutes of clear audio in a quiet environment, reading varied content (not the same sentence repeated).
  4. Write your scripts. Keep individual segments under 60 seconds. Use natural speech patterns — contractions, pauses, conversational phrasing — rather than formal written language.
  5. Integrate with your systems. Connect to your CRM for personalisation, to your phone system or WhatsApp Business API for delivery, and to an analytics platform to measure outcomes.
  6. Test, measure, iterate. Listen to every call in the first week. Identify where customers are confused or frustrated. Fix the script and re-deploy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is voice cloning legal in India?

Yes, voice cloning for legitimate business use is legal in India, provided you have consent from any real person whose voice you are cloning, you disclose to customers that they are interacting with an AI, and you do not use it for fraud or impersonation. India's IT Act and TRAI regulations are evolving to address these issues specifically.

How many Indian languages does voice AI support?

Leading platforms support 8–15 Indian languages including Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Malayalam, and Odia. Sarvam AI, an Indian startup, specifically focuses on Indian language quality. ElevenLabs supports Hindi and several other Indian languages natively.

Can an AI voice sound truly indistinguishable from a human?

In controlled conditions with high-quality audio, modern AI voices score above 90% in human listener tests. However, in phone-quality audio with background noise, experienced listeners can often detect AI voices by subtle patterns in breath sounds and pacing. Disclosure remains both ethical and legally prudent.

What is the minimum audio needed to clone a voice?

ElevenLabs can create a reasonable clone from as little as 1 minute of clean audio, but 5–10 minutes produces significantly better results. For the highest quality — especially for multiple languages — 30–60 minutes of varied, high-quality recordings is ideal.

How do I connect a voice AI to my CRM or existing phone system?

Most voice AI platforms offer API access. Integration with Indian phone systems (Exotel, MyOperator, Knowlarity, Tata Tele) and WhatsApp Business API is straightforward for a developer. Typical integration takes 2–5 days depending on the complexity of your existing infrastructure.
